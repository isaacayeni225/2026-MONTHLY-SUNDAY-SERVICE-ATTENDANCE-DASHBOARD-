# 2026-MONTHLY-SUNDAY-SERVICE-ATTENDANCE-DASHBOARD-
## Overview:

I made this dashboard for my church. The dashboard tracks Sunday service attendance from January to July 2026. It combines a horizontal bar chart, a line chart showing the monthly trend, and a summary table for quick reference. The visual design uses green bars for stronger months and red for lower-attendance months, making performance shifts easy to spot at a glance.

![image alt](https://github.com/isaacayeni225/2026-MONTHLY-SUNDAY-SERVICE-ATTENDANCE-DASHBOARD-/blob/main/Screenshot%20(44).png?raw=true)

Attendance figures:
* January: 1,689  
* February: 1,574  
* March: 1,245  
* April: 1,376  
* May: 1,755  
* June: 1,207  
* July: 1,359  

Total (Jan–Jul): 10,205  
Average monthly attendance: ~1,458

## Key Insights
1. Peak performance in May 
   May recorded the highest attendance of the year so far at 1,755 — a strong recovery and the clear high point of the period.

2. Significant mid-year dip
   Attendance fell sharply from the May peak to 1,207 in June (the lowest month), a drop of nearly 550 people. This was the most pronounced decline in the seven-month window.

3. Early-year downward trend followed by partial recovery 
   Attendance started strong in January (1,689), declined through February and reached a low in March (1,245), then began climbing again in April and May before the June drop.

4. July shows modest rebound
   July attendance rose to 1,359, recovering some ground after the June low but still below the January–May average.

5. Overall pattern
   The data shows volatility rather than a steady trend. Strong months (January, May) are interspersed with weaker periods (March, June). Sustaining the May peak and addressing the factors behind the June decline will be important for the remainder of 2026.

This dashboard provides a clear, data-driven view of attendance patterns and can support planning, follow-up, and continuous improvement efforts at Dominion Porch.
